# WellNū: Intelligent Mobile Nutrition Guide
## Research Study Implementation Plan

### Study Location: Brgy. Looc, Mandaue City, Cebu

---

## General Objective
Systematically analyze, design, and develop WellNū, an intelligent mobile guide to balance nutrition and overall health, helping users monitor food intake and receive AI-driven dietary suggestions for healthier habits and overall well-being.

---

## Specific Objectives & Implementation Status

### 1. Current System Analysis
**Objective 1:** Identify specifics of current system
- **1.1 Processes** ✅ **COMPLETED**
  - Food scanning with AI image recognition
  - Manual food entry and logging
  - Nutrition data tracking (calories, protein, fats, carbs)
  - Daily calorie goal management
  - User profile management with goals
  
- **1.2 Problems Encountered** ✅ **IDENTIFIED**
  - Limited nutritional analysis (only basic macros)
  - No personalized diet recommendations
  - Basic food logging without smart suggestions
  - No real-time imbalance alerts
  - Limited local food database for Filipino cuisine
